Projects

Cauchy's Theorem in Lean

Group project for MA4N1 at the University of Warwick: a formal proof of Cauchy's theorem on a triangle using Goursat's method, built on Mathlib. Includes new definitions of paths, path integrals and triangles, and a fundamental theorem of calculus for complex path integrals.
