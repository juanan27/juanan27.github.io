MSc thesis · Ongoing

Physics-informed neural networks.

University of Seville · Supervisor: Francisco Javier Torres de Lizaur

Computer-Assisted Analysis: the case of PINNs and applications to the De Gregorio model

My thesis uses physics-informed neural networks to explore the linear instability of the De Gregorio model. PINNs helped identify a candidate eigenvalue and guide the search; an analytical argument then validated its existence, establishing a new instability result.

The work also reviews PINN approaches to fluid dynamics and compares their strengths and limitations with spectral methods.

Manuscript in preparation. Thesis defence scheduled for November 2026.
